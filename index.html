Bobbleheads were also handed out live at the game, and the in-app hunt gave fans who couldn't attend a shot too.

One of the 5 hiding spots: Cam tucked into the Team page roster.

The Brief

Give every fan a shot at the exclusive Cam Jordan bobblehead during the March 30–April 3 window, not just the ones who made it into the arena on giveaway night.

The Problem

The physical bobblehead was only handed out live at the game. Fans watching from home had no way to participate or win one.

What I Did

Reskinned the app in the City Edition look, hid 5 bobbleheads throughout the app with a live progress tracker, and built the find-all-5 path into a sweepstakes entry for an autographed bobblehead.

Result Turned a single-night, in-arena giveaway into an app-wide hunt any fan could play and win from. Page views jumped 54% during campaign week, and 185 fans found all 5 bobbleheads and entered the sweepstakes.
MATCHUP 02 · SEASON LAUNCH SAINTS · MOBILE APP

Season Launch: App Rebrand

BEFORE → AFTER · SEASON REBRAND
FULL FIGMA FILE · CLICK THROUGH THE FLOW

The Brief

Redesign the homepage for the new season, introducing a dark mode look and clearer content structure tied to the team's brand refresh.

The Problem

The original homepage was light, flat, and list-based: no visual hierarchy, no sense of occasion, easy to scroll past without engaging.

What I Did

Rebuilt the homepage in dark mode with gold accents and a repeatable card-based system spanning media, schedule, news, videos, and photos, with recurring callouts to gamification and marketing efforts throughout the app. Previewed the direction early with a special dark mode takeover for NFL Draft night.

Result A homepage that reads as unmistakably "new season" at a glance, with a sectioned structure that's easier to scan, plus a draft night teaser that built anticipation before the full rebrand shipped.
